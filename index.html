CURRICULAR INTEGRATION

A five-semester engineering progression

The Experiential Learning Centre is Thapar Institute's core engineering proving ground, operating 60 central power workbenches and three specialized maker studios. Here, engineering undergraduates advance beyond simulated models to design, mill, solder, and deploy operational machines across their degree.

Every undergraduate discipline completes required experiential coursework each term, moving systematically from fundamental hand tools to complex multi-disciplinary systems before capstone execution.

Drive-by-wire test platform

An electrified light vehicle testbed built with full drive-by-wire steering, braking, and throttle control. The vehicle serves as the real-time proving ground for student sensor fusion algorithms combining dual 16-channel LiDAR and differential GPS.
