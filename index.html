CENTRE CHARTER

Hands-on physical engineering

The Experiential Learning Centre is Thapar Institute's core engineering proving ground, operating three specialized maker studios. Here, engineering undergraduates advance beyond simulated models to design, mill, solder, and deploy operational machines across their degree.

NON-CREDIT DESIGN CURRICULUM

Five semesters of machine building

Every undergraduate discipline completes required experiential coursework each term, moving systematically from basic hand tools to advanced multi-disciplinary robotics and power systems.

SEM I

Fundamental Tools

Tools, CAD, chemical PCB etching, Arduino motor control.

SEM II

Robotics & Vision

Multi-axis robotic arms, mobile app lifecycle, OpenCV.

SEM III

Systems & Power

Edge/cloud IoT, firewall defense, linear power supplies, DC motor rewinding.

SEM IV

Kinematics & ML

Wearable ML, ethical hacking, 4-DOF servo kinematics.

SEM V

Advanced Synthesis

FPGA HDL clocks, switch-mode inverters, vision/voice robots.

See the five semesters

AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS · SENSOR FUSION

Drive-by-wire test platform

An electrified light vehicle testbed built with full drive-by-wire steering, braking, and throttle control. The vehicle serves as the real-time proving ground for student sensor fusion algorithms combining dual 16-channel LiDAR and differential GPS.
