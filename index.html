EDUCATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

The Cornerstone to Capstone Model.

Established at TIET in January 2018 under the guidance of institute leadership, ELC was created to replace prescriptive laboratory routines with authentic, open-ended engineering problem-solving.

Integrated into undergraduate engineering programs without additional academic credit pressure, students have the freedom to explore physical design, make constructive errors, and build working systems before entering their final-year Capstone projects.

Outcome-Based Accreditation Standards: Aligned with ABET & Washington Accord requirements.
01

Early Discipline Immersion

Semesters 1 & 2

Direct hands-on experience with CNC laser cutting, chemical PCB etching, thermodynamic pressure apparatus, and Arduino microcontrollers within the first year of college.

02

Multidisciplinary Systems Integration

Semesters 3, 4 & 5

Interdisciplinary teams tackle complex engineering design: edge IoT telemetry, municipal wastewater material balance, FPGA digital synthesis on Xilinx Vivado, and 4-DOF servo kinematics.

03

Summer Intensive Fellowships

Pre-Capstone Incubation

Full-time campus research cohorts building functional prototypes: electric vehicle conversions, satellite camera gas chambers, and hospital triage carts.

CURRICULAR FRAMEWORK

The 5-Semester Experiential Arc.

Embedded across all undergraduate branches: Computer Science, Chemical, Electronics & Communication, and Electrical & Instrumentation.

STAGE 01

Semester I

Tools exploration, CAD basics, chemical PCB etching, thermodynamic air behavior, and Arduino motor control.

STAGE 02

Semester II

Multi-axis robotic arms, mobile application lifecycle, overhead storage tanks (378 m³), and OpenCV shape detection.

STAGE 03

Semester III

Edge & cloud IoT, network firewall defense, textile dye adsorption, linear power supplies, and DC motor rewinding.

STAGE 04

Semester IV

Healthcare wearable machine learning, ethical hacking, industrial wastewater plants, and 4-DOF servo kinematics.

STAGE 05

Semester V

Vision/voice telekinesis bots, Soxhlet oil extraction, FPGA HDL digital clocks on Xilinx Vivado, and switch-mode inverters.
