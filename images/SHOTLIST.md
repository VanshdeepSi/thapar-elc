# ELC Homepage Photography Shotlist
**Experiential Learning Centre (ELC) · Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala**

This document specifies the required photographic assets for the ELC homepage. Every photo must feature authentic student engineering work inside Block B workshops or active campus test locations. **No stock photography, staged corporate poses, or AI-generated imagery are permitted.**

---

### Technical Requirements for All Deliverables:
- **Minimum Resolution:** 2000px wide on the shortest dimension (RAW or high-bitrate TIFF/PNG preferred, exported as sRGB 85% JPEG).
- **Lighting:** Natural laboratory ambient lighting complemented by task lighting (warm white workbench tubes, LED strip ring lights). Avoid heavy artificial flash shadows.
- **Depth of Field:** Moderate depth of field ($f/2.8$ to $f/5.6$) keeping the hardware in sharp focus with student team members active in the workspace context.
- **Color Grading:** True-to-life tones, neutral white balance calibrated to laboratory workbench surfaces.
